**Description:**

YouputDesk is a free, open-source desktop app for YouTube Music on Windows. It runs the real YouTube Music, so
your account, library and Premium work as usual, and adds what the browser can't: a 10-band equalizer with a
limiter, synced lyrics, themes (including one that follows the album art), a tray mini player, global hotkeys,
ad alerts and Discord status. No accounts, no analytics.

**Maker's first comment:**

Hi Product Hunt! I built YouputDesk because I listen to YouTube Music all day and wanted an EQ, synced lyrics and
hotkeys that work from any app. It's MIT licensed and built in the open, and it's private by default: nothing
leaves your PC until you allow it. I'd love to hear what you'd add next.
