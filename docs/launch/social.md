**Post 1:**

I built YouputDesk: a free desktop app for YouTube Music on Windows.

10-band EQ, synced lyrics, themes, a tray mini player and global hotkeys. Your account and Premium work as usual.

Open source, no tracking.
https://roderio.github.io/youputdesk/

**Post 2 (reply):**

The EQ runs through Web Audio with a limiter at the end, so bass boost never distorts. Lyrics come from LRCLIB
and scroll in time with the song. Click a line to jump there.

**Post 3 (reply):**

Install with `winget install roderio.YouputDesk`, or from Scoop. Windows 10/11, x64 and ARM.
Source: https://github.com/roderio/youputdesk
