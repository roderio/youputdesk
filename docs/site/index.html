YouTube Music, with the controls you wished it had.

A free Windows app that runs the real YouTube Music and adds an equalizer, synced lyrics, themes, a tray mini player and global hotkeys. Your account and Premium work as usual.

Download for Windows

For Windows 10 and 11, on x64 and ARM. Free and open source.

Make it yours

Pick a built-in palette, let the Album Art theme follow the cover, or build your own in the theme editor and share it as a file.

Control it from anywhere

Global hotkeys work from any app, and every shortcut can be rebound, with a warning if it clashes with another. Media keys and the taskbar buttons work too.

Know when an ad starts

A banner and a Windows notification tell you when an ad plays, and one key skips it whenever YouTube allows. High contrast, reduced motion and screen reader announcements are built in.

  • Synced lyrics. Lines scroll in time with the song. Click one to jump there.
  • Tray and mini player. Close the window and the music keeps going.
  • Discord status. Show friends what you're listening to.
  • Private by default. No accounts, no analytics. Nothing leaves your PC until you say so.

Install

Download the installer and open it. It installs in a few seconds without admin rights and keeps itself up to date.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

In PowerShell or Terminal:

winget install roderio.YouputDesk

Update later with winget upgrade roderio.YouputDesk, or let the app update itself.

In PowerShell:

scoop bucket add roderio https://github.com/roderio/scoop-bucket
scoop install youputdesk

Your sign-in and settings are kept across scoop update.

Download the portable exe and run it from anywhere, even a USB stick. It keeps its data in a YouputDesk-data folder beside it and doesn't update itself.

Questions

Is it safe? Windows warned me about it.

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognize it. Every release is built by GitHub Actions from the public source code, and signing through the SignPath Foundation is on the way. Installing with winget or Scoop skips the warning.

Does YouTube Music Premium work?

Yes. YouputDesk shows the real music.youtube.com, so you sign in with your Google account just like in a browser.

Does it block ads?

No. It tells you when an ad starts and lets you skip it with one key whenever YouTube shows a Skip button.

What does it send, and to whom?

Nothing until you choose. On first launch it asks about each optional feature that talks to another service: Discord status, online lyrics and update checks. The privacy policy lists exactly what goes where.

Something stopped working.

YouTube Music changes its page now and then. Report it and the fix reaches you through the automatic updater.
